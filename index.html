About Me

The Story
Behind the Work

With over seven years of experience in customer-facing roles, I have built a strong foundation in communication, problem-solving, and delivering high-quality service experiences.

Driven by curiosity and a passion for digital creation, I transitioned into web design and development as a self-built professional — independently learning, building, and refining real-world websites and business solutions.

I specialize in creating modern, clean, and strategic websites that are not only visually compelling but also functional and conversion-focused. My background in customer service gives me a unique advantage: I understand user behavior, client needs, and how to design experiences that build trust and drive results.

I may not come from a traditional tech background, but what I bring is discipline, adaptability, and a proven ability to learn fast, execute effectively, and deliver real value.
